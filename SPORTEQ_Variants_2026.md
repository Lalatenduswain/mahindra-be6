# Mahindra BE 6 (2026) - SPORTEQ Variant Details

### Key Takeaways for Buyers:
1. **The Sweet Spot:** The **SPORTEQ Two** is likely the best value for tech lovers on a budget. It introduces the signature three-screen dashboard and 540-degree camera while still being eligible for the ultra-low BaaS pricing model.
2. **The Comfort Pick:** The **SPORTEQ Three** is where premium physical comforts begin (Ventilated seats and Powered driver seat). 
3. **The Tech Flagship:** You have to step up to the **Three+** for Level 2 ADAS. The top-tier **Four** is the only one packing the ultra-fast Snapdragon 8295 processor and the Augmented Reality Heads-Up Display.
